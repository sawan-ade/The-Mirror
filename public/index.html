What the Mirror does

🌌
Galaxy Map
Interactive constellation — your concepts as stars, relationships as light threads between them.
📸
Photo Memory NEW
Attach images to any concept node. Visual memories anchored to your ideas.
🎙️
Voice Notes NEW
Record a voice note for any concept. Hear your thoughts back in your own voice.
📅
Timeline View NEW
Scroll through the evolution of your thinking over time. See ideas grow, fade, and transform.
◈
Hidden Connections
The AI reveals non-obvious bridges between concepts you never consciously connected.
🖼️
Share Card NEW
Export a beautiful poster of your thought galaxy to share with the world.
◎
Forgotten Ideas
Highlights concepts that burned bright then quietly vanished from your writing.
✦
Ask the Mirror
Ask natural-language questions. Get answers grounded in your own words as evidence.
